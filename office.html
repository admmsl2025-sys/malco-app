Open Malco Office

Put Malco Office on your home screen

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or another app).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (on iPad, top right).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you can't see it, tap Edit Actions first.
  4. Tap Add. The Malco icon appears on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Add or Install. The Malco icon appears on your home screen.

From now on, just tap the icon. You sign in with your Staff ID and PIN as usual.
